About DMI Technologies

Company

Two Decades of Connectivity Expertise

DMI Technologies was founded in 2005 as a women-owned technology company, specializing in structured cabling installations. Since then, DMI has continually expanded its offering, now also providing electronic security, nurse-call, mobility solutions, and patient alarm management.

From certified technicians to our Project Management and Sales teams, every employee at DMI is passionate about leveraging technology to enhance safety, security, and communication in healthcare and commercial environments — and every project is backed by our Texas DPS license.

DMI has experience designing, installing, and servicing a variety of commercial markets including Healthcare, Higher Education, K-12, and Multi-tenant spaces for both public and private companies.
At DMI, we take pride in our team of highly trained and experienced professionals who are dedicated to delivering exceptional service in the fields of nurse-call, electronic security, and structured cabling. With a workforce of over 85 skilled individuals, each member of our team brings a wealth of expertise and a commitment to excellence. From our certified technicians to our Project Management and Sales team, every employee at DMI is passionate about leveraging technology to enhance safety, security, and communication in healthcare and commercial environments. Together, we strive to exceed our clients' expectations and uphold our reputation as a trusted leader in the industry.
At DMI, we're honored to serve clients in both Texas and Colorado. With a strong foothold in these dynamic regions, our dedicated team brings expertise in nurse-call systems, electronic security, and structured cabling to meet the diverse needs of our clients. Whether it's ensuring healthcare facilities in Texas have reliable communication systems or providing cutting-edge security solutions for businesses in Colorado, we're committed to delivering excellence every step of the way.

DMI is licensed by the Texas Department of Public Safety Regulatory Services Division.

Multi-Tenant Solutions

The communications design plan for a multi-tenant building is vital to the success of the property. A mistake during this phase could lead to disastrous connectivity issues and costly retro-fit work for tenants. DMI has helped many developers with design assist and full implementations of communications, multi-media, distributed antenna systems, and security solutions.

Our team offers advancements in technology that increase sellable space and reduce operating expense for the entire building.
